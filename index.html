How to use GUARDIAN

• Tap EMERGENCY SOS or shake your phone or triple-tap the screen to trigger safety mode.
• When asked “Are you safe?”, tap NO if you are in danger. Help mode will activate automatically.
• If you do nothing for 5 seconds, GUARDIAN will assume danger and activate emergency mode.

Do’s
• Use GUARDIAN only in real emergency situations.
• Keep your phone accessible when going out alone.
• Test the system before using it in real life.

Don’ts
• Do not misuse or create fake alerts.
• Do not trigger emergency for fun or jokes.
• Respect that this system is meant to protect lives.
